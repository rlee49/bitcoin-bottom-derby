# v34 Realistic Alternate Test

This is a separate experimental branch from the locked v32 final/master build.

## What changed
- Replaced the five on-track racer renderings with realistic alternate art assets.
- Rodster remains a black-armored knight on a black warhorse.
- Tatiana remains a dark-haired female rider in black/pink on a black horse.
- Bike now rides the requested horse/bicycle hybrid with wheels, chain and pedals, with no cape.
- Tom remains the corn/cowboy themed donkey rider with carrot-on-a-stick.
- WhiteSw0n now has the white fuzzy llama/alpaca with a female rider with very light blonde/white hair and teal/white gear.
- Replaced the top race backdrop with a denser realistic crypto crowd and snow-capped mountain scene.
- Kept the v33 compact broadcast layout, Bookie Board, Discord entry preview, Race Info, Paddock Picks, Bitcoin Depth Meter and race logic.

## Test-build note
This is the first functional realistic-art integration pass. The racer art uses lightweight in-place motion rather than full multi-frame photorealistic gallop cycles. The purpose of v34 is to test the look, scale, layout and usability before generating/curating full motion cycles.

The locked v32 final/master build was not modified.
