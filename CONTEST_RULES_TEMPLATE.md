## 2. Free entry

No purchase, payment, deposit, or cryptocurrency transfer is required. Each eligible person may submit one free horse pick.

## 3. Race winner

The official Bitcoin contest low is the lowest BTC/USD trade recorded by the designated price source during the contest period.

The winning horse is the prediction target with the smallest absolute difference from the official contest low. An exact target touch is not required. Exact equal differences are a dead heat and must be handled under the tie rule below.

Targets:

- Tom — $59,000 contingency low; primary statement “bottom is in”
- Tatiana — $53,300 bullish-divergence low; primary statement “bottom is in”
- Rodster — $47,976
- Bike — $38,750

## 4. Prize method

Recommended version: One entrant who selected the winning horse is chosen at random to receive $100 worth of TAO, valued at the sponsor’s stated purchase or transfer time.

Tie option: If two horses are an exact dead heat, combine eligible entries on both horses into one drawing.

## 5. Claim process

The selected entrant must provide [contact method], eligibility confirmation, and a valid TAO destination address if required. Set a claim deadline and a redraw process for an unclaimed or invalid prize.

## 6. Important disclosures

State age and location eligibility, privacy terms, publicity permissions, tax responsibility, exclusions, technical-failure rules, and the designated BTC/USD data source. Explain that displayed probability and bookie odds are entertainment estimates and do not guarantee the outcome.
